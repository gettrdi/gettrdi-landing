The research engine

We handle the research. You make the calls.

This data is public — but scattered across hundreds of government portals, review platforms, and filing systems. Most contractors never see it. Those who do rarely act fast enough. trdi aggregates it, uses ML to score for buying intent, and turns it into a call-ready brief.

Health & safety inspections

Violations at food facilities and commercial properties create an immediate window for pest, cleaning, HVAC, and fire services.

Building permits

Active renovation permits mean spend is in motion and no contractor is locked in yet. The window is open.

Code violations & notices

A cited property needs fast remediation. The owner is already looking — your call lands when it matters most.

Business filings & changes

New registrations and ownership transfers mean fresh decision-makers with no existing vendor — and an open door.

Licensing & compliance gaps

Expiring certifications and upcoming deadlines create urgency. Your call arrives with a ready reason to act.

Google Business Profile

Rating drops, repeated service complaints, and flagged Q&A reveal accounts with active pain your service can solve.

Yelp & review platforms

Health badge changes, star dips, and recurring complaint patterns surface businesses that need help and know it.

Nextdoor & local forums

Neighborhood complaints and service callouts identify local businesses with reputation problems you can directly fix.

Property records

Assessor data, recent sales, and ownership changes flag properties likely to be under-serviced and actively managed.
